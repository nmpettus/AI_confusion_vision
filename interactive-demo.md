# Combined Interactive Demo: Perturbations + Edge Cases (Ages 6–12)

## Goal
Teach kids that:
- **Small picture changes** can confuse AI (**perturbations**).
- **Rare situations** can confuse AI (**edge cases**).
- **Sometimes they overlap** when changes are big or unusual.

The experience is playful, visual, and quick to understand.

---

## Audience
- Children ages **6–12**
- Parents/teachers who want simple, accurate language

---

## Demo Overview (3 Mini-Zones)

### 1) **Green Zone: “Practice Zone”**
**Purpose:** Show AI works well on normal images.

**Interaction:**
- Kid chooses a **normal picture** (cat, stop sign, apple, face).
- AI identifies it **correctly** with a happy sound.

**Micro-copy (on screen):**
> “AI practiced on pictures like this.”

---

### 2) **Yellow Zone: “Small Changes”**
**Purpose:** Show small input changes (perturbations).

**Interaction:**
- Sliders: **Blur**, **Brightness**, **Rotation**, **Noise**.
- Kid adjusts a tiny amount.
- AI still gets it right (most of the time).

**Micro-copy:**
> “The picture changed a little.”

---

### 3) **Red Zone: “Edge Cases”**
**Purpose:** Show rare or unusual situations.

**Interaction:**
- Buttons: **Snow on sign**, **Costume**, **Mirror reflection**, **Half in shadow**.
- AI may get confused or guess wrong.

**Micro-copy:**
> “This is a rare situation. AI didn’t practice this much.”

---

## The Overlap (Crossover Moment)

### **“Yellow → Red” Transition**
**Purpose:** Show how perturbations can become edge cases.

**Interaction:**
- Kid increases blur or darkness to a “too much” level.
- The zone meter shifts from **Yellow** to **Red**.
- AI’s confidence drops or mislabels.

**Micro-copy:**
> “When a change is big or rare, it becomes an edge case.”

---

## Single-Sentence Teaching Lines (Use Exactly)
- **“AI is trained for the common case — not every case.”**
- **“An edge case is a rare situation that AI wasn’t ready for.”**
- **“Small changes are perturbations. Big or rare changes are edge cases.”**

---

## Characters (Optional but Recommended)

### Maggie (helper)
- Encouraging tone
- Explains what is happening

### Artie (confused)
- Shows when the AI is unsure
- Helps kids notice mistakes

**Example dialog:**
- Maggie: “That’s a normal picture. AI knows this!”
- Artie: “Uh-oh, this picture is weird. I’m not sure!”

---

## UI Layout (Simple)

- **Left panel:** Image playground
- **Right panel:**
  - AI guess + confidence bar
  - Zone indicator (Green → Yellow → Red)
  - One-line explanation

---

## Kid-Friendly Rule of Thumb (Display as a Sticker)
> “When pictures change a little, AI can still guess.
> When pictures change a lot or in weird ways, it becomes an edge case.”

---

## Summary Card (End Screen)
- ✅ **Perturbations** = small changes to a picture
- ✅ **Edge cases** = rare situations AI didn’t practice
- ✅ **Overlap** = big changes that make the situation rare

**Final line:**
> “You helped AI learn its limits!”
